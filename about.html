About

Digital Transformation Specialist

I help organizations modernize the systems, workflows, and customer experiences that are holding them back — combining digital transformation strategy, AI-enabled modernization, and enterprise design thinking to turn legacy pain points into measurable business outcomes.

Over the past several years I've led end-to-end transformation initiatives across banking, government-linked transit infrastructure, and healthcare access — working directly with executive stakeholders, engineering teams, and operations leads to move organizations from fragmented, manual processes to unified digital platforms. As Senior UX Designer at CGI Inc, I bring that same rigor to enterprise clients navigating large-scale modernization programs.

My approach treats design as a transformation lever, not a finishing coat: I start from the business problem and operating constraints, apply structured discovery and stakeholder alignment, then use AI-enabled tooling and modern interface patterns to accelerate delivery without losing sight of the people who have to use the system every day.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Studies and a Master's degree in Sustainable Development from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria. I am an IBM Enterprise Design Thinking Practitioner and completed Digify Byte's Google-backed social media strategy program.
